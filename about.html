About STROKES

STROKES is the official Art and Photography club of NIT Durgapur. Established with the vision to foster creativity and provide a platform for artistic expression, we have grown into a vibrant community of artists and photographers.

We organize workshops, exhibitions, and competitions throughout the year, encouraging students to explore their creative side amidst their academic pursuits. From capturing the subtle plays of light to expressing emotions on canvas, STROKES is where engineering meets art.
